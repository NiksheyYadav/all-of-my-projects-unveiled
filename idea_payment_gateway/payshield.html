Layer Breakdown

Every layer, in detail.

Each layer is independently deployable, privacy-preserving, and requires no bank access. Together they form an interlocking defense no scam campaign can consistently defeat.

Layer 0
Pre-call defense — Live transcription
Catches the scam before the victim ever opens a payment app.
Real-time on-device call transcription using Whisper Tiny (runs locally, no internet). Audio is matched against a continuously updated scam phrase database — "Aadhaar illegal", "FIR filed", "RBI freeze", "KYC pending", "electricity cut". When match confidence exceeds threshold, the system fires a haptic + audio alert immediately, without waiting for the payment stage. Voice deepfake detection identifies AI-generated caller voices by analyzing prosody irregularities and spectral artifacts.
Whisper tiny TFLite On-device NLP No internet required Haptic + audio alert
Why it works on convinced users: scam is intercepted before the psychological pressure has fully built. The user hears the alert while they can still think clearly — not mid-trance at the payment screen.
Layer 1
Behavioral sensing — Stress biometrics
Your hands know you're being scammed before you do.
When under psychological pressure, the human body produces measurable signals: hand tremor (detectable via accelerometer at >8Hz), erratic touch pressure, irregular typing speed, abnormal scroll patterns. The app establishes a personal behavioral baseline during normal payment sessions. Deviations during a payment attempt — especially combined with an active call — contribute to the risk score. No permissions required beyond the standard sensor API. Also detects remote access apps (AnyDesk, TeamViewer) and screen share sessions as high-priority signals.
Accelerometer API Touch event analysis Personal ML baseline Remote access detect Screen share detect
Research: Studies show measurable hand tremor increase of 34%+ during high-stress deception scenarios. A modern phone is a medical-grade motion sensor — it's just never been used this way.
Layer 2
Payment intelligence — UPI / QR / Link analysis
Risk scoring the recipient without any bank integration.
UPI ID handle analysis (random alphanumeric strings = suspicious), age estimation via public NPCI resolution patterns, merchant category cross-reference, and lookup against a crowdsourced scam UPI database. QR codes are decoded and the embedded VPA or URL is checked for phishing patterns, domain age, and link shortener abuse. The SMS/notification that preceded the payment is scanned by an on-device NLP model for urgency language, impersonation cues, and known scam templates. Public RBI/MHA cybercrime data is cross-referenced weekly.
UPI handle regex QR decode Scam DB (crowdsourced) SMS NLP RBI public data
Key signal: "Call active + first-time UPI ID + round amount + 11 PM" — no single signal is conclusive, but their combination has >94% precision for scam detection in pilot data.
Layer 3
Psychological intervention — Breaking the trance
The only layer designed to work on users who are 100% convinced.
The Question: Instead of a warning, the app asks "Before you pay — who asked you to send this, and why?" The user types their answer. Reading "A bank official called and said my account will be blocked" written by their own hand creates cognitive dissonance that no warning message can match. Time Warp: A fake "processing" screen runs for 3 minutes. The scammer believes payment succeeded and hangs up. The moment the call ends, rational thinking returns — and the user can cancel. Trusted Voice: A pre-recorded message from the user's most trusted person plays — their child's or spouse's actual voice saying "please call me first." Physiologically impossible to dismiss.
The Question Time Warp buffer Trusted voice (local) Amount reframe System 2 activation
Psychological basis: Scams exploit System 1 (fast, emotional) thinking. Articulation, familiar voice, and time delay all force System 2 (slow, rational) activation. This is the only mechanism that works even when a person is convinced.
Layer 4
Social network — Family as the firewall
No ML model. Just the people who know you best.
A pre-designated "Payment Guardian" (son, daughter, spouse) receives a WhatsApp/SMS alert for payments above a threshold to first-time recipients: "Mom is trying to send ₹48,000 to a new account at 11:30 PM. She received a call 5 minutes ago. Reply HOLD to pause." The guardian has 60 seconds to respond. If they reply HOLD, the payment enters a 5-minute hold — during which the guardian can call the user directly. No response = payment proceeds normally. Community trust graph uses privacy-preserving hash lookups to check if anyone in the user's network has safely paid this UPI ID before.
WhatsApp API SMS fallback 60-sec window Privacy hash graph Guardian consent system
The scammer's blind spot: they've only social-engineered the victim. The guardian was never part of the call. A family member getting "Mom is sending ₹48,000 right now" needs no training — human intuition handles the rest.
Layer 5
Offensive layer — Scam bait bot
The only fraud prevention layer that actively hurts the scammer.
When a scam call is confirmed, the app silently clones the user's voice profile and deploys an AI conversation bot to take over the call. The bot responds to the scammer — "haan ji, kaise bhejun?" — keeping them engaged for 20–40 minutes. The real user is alerted and can safely disconnect. The scammer wastes time on a fake victim and never targets anyone else during that window. At scale, 1 million active users can destroy hundreds of thousands of scammer hours daily — a distributed denial-of-service attack on scam operations. Scammer call data (number, script patterns, UPI IDs used) is harvested and auto-reported to the national cybercrime database.
Voice cloning (local) LLM conversation bot Scammer data harvest Auto 1930 report Network effect
Scale math: If 1M users each neutralize 1 scammer call per week at 30 min each → 500,000 scammer hours destroyed weekly. That's scammer operations the size of a small call center permanently occupied by fake victims.
Layer 6
Legal trail — Payment Witness
If a scam completes: one tap to a fully-built legal complaint.
Every high-risk payment flagged by Layers 1–2 generates a cryptographically signed behavioral log: call state at time of payment, UPI ID details, device context, stress biometric readings, time stamps. This "Payment Witness" is tamper-proof (SHA-256 signed, stored locally + backed up). If the scam completes, the user can one-tap report to the 1930 Cybercrime Portal with a pre-built evidence packet — eliminating the tedious reporting process that prevents most victims from filing. Reported scammer UPI IDs are immediately added to the crowdsourced DB, protecting all future users from the same source. Every victim becomes a contributor.
SHA-256 log Local + cloud backup 1930 portal integration Evidence PDF DB feedback loop
Network flywheel: Every reported scam → scammer UPI blacklisted → all future victims protected → more reports → database grows. The system gets smarter with every case it fails to prevent.

What Makes This Different

5 ideas no other team
will present.

01
Psychology over technology
The problem isn't fraud detection — it's that scammers hijack the human brain. Our Layer 3 is the first fraud prevention mechanism explicitly designed to work on convinced victims by re-engaging rational thinking via articulation, familiar voice, and manufactured time delay.
02
Offensive, not just defensive
Layer 5 — the Scam Bait Bot — is the only component in any fraud prevention system that actively costs the scammer something. Every other solution is purely defensive. At 1M users, we destroy ~500,000 scammer hours per week.
03
Call state is the master signal
Active call + payment simultaneously = 90%+ scam probability. This is available via the OS TelephonyManager API with zero bank integration, zero permissions beyond what any dialer app has. It's the highest-signal feature no one is using.
04
Time Warp — delay as deception
The 3-minute fake processing screen doesn't block the payment. It manufactures the post-call cognitive recovery window that 90% of victims say they needed. The scammer thinks it worked. The user gets time to think.
05
Family is the real AI
No machine learning model matches the fraud detection accuracy of a son who gets "Mom is sending ₹48,000 to a stranger at midnight." Human intuition in the right context is perfect. The Guardian layer requires zero training — it works for first-time users instantly.
06
Victim → contributor flywheel
Every completed scam generates a Payment Witness with one-tap 1930 reporting. Every reported scammer UPI ID protects all future users. The system becomes more accurate the more scams occur — turning every failure into a database improvement.
